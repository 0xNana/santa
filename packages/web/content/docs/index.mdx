**Send stocks to the people you love.**

Santa is an RWA (real-world assets) stock-gifting experience. Celebrate a birthday, mark a milestone, or send a little “just because” with a stock gift and a personal note.

Connect an Ethereum wallet, choose a stock you already hold, and decide how much to give. Santa wraps it in a private gift link. Your loved one opens the link and signs in with Google or email; a wallet is created for them, and Santa pays the claim fee.

**Currently on testnet.** Gifts use testnet stock tokens with no monetary value or equity ownership rights. Santa is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain Testnet.

Start with [getting started](/docs/getting-started), see [how gifts work](/docs/how-it-works), or use the [CLI](/docs/agent).
