## What can I gift with Santa?

Santa lets you send stocks to loved ones as a private gift link, with a personal note. The current vault supports TSLA, AMD, AMZN, NFLX, and PLTR on Robinhood Chain Testnet. You gift stock you already hold; Santa does not buy it for you.

## What does testnet mean for my stock gift?

The current experience uses testnet stock tokens on Robinhood Chain Testnet. They let you try the complete stock-gifting flow, but have no monetary value or equity ownership rights.

## Who pays fees?

The sender pays test ETH for approvals and gift creation. Santa’s relayer pays test ETH for claims. There is no wrapping fee.

## Does the recipient need a wallet?

No wallet app is required. Google or email sign-in creates a Privy embedded wallet. The recipient still signs a message proving that the wallet is theirs.

## What if the link is lost?

Santa’s server cannot recover the secret key. The sender’s browser or CLI saves it locally before sending. If all copies are lost, the sender can refund after expiry.

## Can a gift expire?

Yes. After expiry, a refund returns an unclaimed gift to its original sender. A refund must be requested; it is not automatic.

## Why are some requests refused by location?

Santa retains the existing conservative region and declaration checks during this migration. These are application controls, not claims about eligibility for Robinhood products.

## Is Santa affiliated with Robinhood?

Santa is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain Testnet.
